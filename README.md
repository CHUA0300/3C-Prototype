# 3C · Custom Control Content: interactive prototypes

**Shaping the future of digital content safety. Safety powered by 3C.**

3C is a family digital-safety concept that uses AI to stop scams, deepfakes and harmful content *before* they reach the people we love — kids and elderly parents — while giving the family alerts, not surveillance.

These are clickable prototypes built for a group venture proposal. They run entirely in the browser with demo data; nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

## How to demo (about 60 seconds each)

**v0 · Tap to check**
1. On Mdm Tan’s phone, tap **Check through 3C** on the “Ministry Officials” video. Watch the five layers run and the deepfake get caught.
2. Check the **PayNow request**: the payment is paused for 10 minutes.
3. Open **My rewards**: Mdm Tan can now redeem 1GB of mobile data.
4. On Rachel’s dashboard, open a **post-mortem report**.

**v1 · Automatic**
1. Use the **Play out the day** buttons on the left. Mdm Tan presses nothing.
2. Tap a scam link in an SMS: the page is stopped before it opens.
3. Visit the supermarket site: it opens normally, checked silently.
4. Click **Ethan switches 3C off**: Rachel gets a tamper alert.

## The five-layer filter

Cheap checks run first, so the expensive one only runs when needed.

1. **Blocklist:** known scam numbers and links
2. **Machine learning:** scores messages and links for scam risk
3. **Deep learning:** detects deepfake video and voice (only when media is present)
4. **Generative AI:** explains the result in plain language: educate, don’t scold
5. **Tracking:** logs the check to the family scorecard

## Design principles

- **Alerts, not surveillance:** the buyer sees threats, never the family’s chats
- **Consent first:** protection is switched on with the user’s agreement
- **Dignity:** explanations instead of blocks, so seniors keep face
- **Age-tiered controls:** teen settings loosen automatically as they grow
