How it works

Five layers, cheapest first

  1. 1
    BlocklistKnown scam numbers and links
  2. 2
    Machine learningScores messages and links for scam risk
  3. 3
    Deep learningDetects deepfake video and voice, only when media is present
  4. 4
    Generative AIExplains the result in plain language
  5. 5
    TrackingLogs each check to the family scorecard

The expensive deepfake check only runs when the cheaper layers can’t clear the content.

Design principles

Built to be accepted, not bypassed

Alerts, not surveillanceThe buyer sees threats, never the family’s chats.
Consent firstProtection is switched on with the user’s agreement.
DignityExplain, don’t scold, so seniors keep face.
Age-tiered controlsTeen settings loosen automatically as they grow.
